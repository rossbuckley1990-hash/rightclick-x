## How the goal works
- **Steps:** a live countries data source looks up the code PT and returns capital "Lisbon". Wikipedia's page summary for that capital is then fetched. Finally, the environment's formatter (`renderdestinationbrief`) gets the capital as `place` and the summary as `text`.
- **Input:** only `country` (type `country`). No capital, summary, URL or expected answer is fixed in the goal. **Output:** `markdown`.
- **Permissions:** limited to read-only operations.
- **Audit:** the independent auditor (`openapi:independent-live-destination-auditor:auditdestinationbrief`) is a separate service. It is saved as a required check on every run, with `country` taken from the goal's input and `candidate` taken from the goal's full output. It must return `valid` equal to `true`.
